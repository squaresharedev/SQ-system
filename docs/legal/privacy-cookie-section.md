## Cookies, similar technologies, and personalization

### Cookies and trackers

We use cookies and similar technologies (including `localStorage`) in four categories: **strictly necessary** (sign-in, cross-subdomain single sign-on, checkout fraud prevention, security, and the consent cookie itself — always active), and, **only with your consent**, **functional/preferences**, **analytics**, and **personalization**. Nothing non-essential runs on your device before you make an explicit choice in the consent banner, and you can withdraw your choice at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in every page footer — withdrawal takes effect immediately.

The full list of every cookie and tracker — name, provider, specific purpose, duration, first-/third-party status, and the personal data involved — is in our [Cookie Policy]([PLACEHOLDER: Cookie Policy URL — assumed /legal/cookie-policy]).

**Legal bases:** consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for functional, analytics and personalization storage; contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest in security (Art. 6(1)(f)) for the processing behind strictly necessary items.

**Recipients:** our processors for these purposes are Supabase (authentication/database), Cloudflare (security/delivery), Stripe (payments/fraud prevention), and PostHog (analytics, consent-gated). See the Cookie Policy for links to their policies.

**International transfers:** some processors may process data in the United States, relying on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is self-certified and/or Standard Contractual Clauses ([PLACEHOLDER: DPF self-certification status for Supabase, Stripe, PostHog]; Cloudflare is DPF-certified).

### Personalization and recommendations (profiling)

When you use Square Share while signed in, our servers record your product interactions (for example items viewed, likes, follows, saves, and purchases) **server-side only** — no cookie or device identifier is used for this. We use these records to rank and recommend content within Square Share: **the logic** is that items similar to those you interacted with are ranked higher in your feed; **the consequence** is that the content you see is ordered differently than for other users. This involves no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR).

This processing rests on our **legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in operating a relevant marketplace. You have the **right to object at any time** (Art. 21 GDPR): use the personalization opt-out in your settings or contact **[PLACEHOLDER: privacy contact email]** — after which we stop using your activity for personalization and you receive a non-personalized experience.
